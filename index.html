How to use this guide

Four steps to a solid trip

Work top to bottom, then deepen each city page. Official Japan National Tourism Organization resources stay one click away.

1

Pick cities

Compare 14 destinations with distances from Tokyo, seasons, and stay length.

 2

Shape a route

Use 5–14 day arcs or theme trips (food, nature, sakura) as a starting skeleton.

 3

Solve transport

IC cards, shinkansen basics, and when a JR Pass or regional pass may make sense.

 4

Run the numbers

Currency, tax, rail estimates, and emergency numbers in Travel Tools.

Fares, pass rules, and entry requirements change. Confirm with JR, airlines, and japan.travel (JNTO) before booking.
