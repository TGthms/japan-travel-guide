Useful numbers in Japan

Save these before you need them.

  • 110 — Police
  • 119 — Fire & ambulance
  • 118 — Coast Guard
  • 0570-000-911 — Japan Helpline (English-friendly)
  • #7119 — Non-emergency medical advice (many areas)
